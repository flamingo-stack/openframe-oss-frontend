### `TicketDialogSubscription`
A render-less component (`returns null`) over the core lib's JetStream tail (`jetStreamLiveTail`) on the client topic (`NATS_TOPICS.MESSAGE`): it opens from `clientInitialOptStartSeq` once `isInitialOptStartSeqReady` is true, drops a redelivered chunk by its stream sequence (the lib's redelivery gate), and calls `onCaughtUp` when the tail came back after a gap.

```tsx
<TicketDialogSubscription
  dialogId={dialogId}
  dispatchChunk={processChunk}
  clientInitialOptStartSeq={startSeq}
  isInitialOptStartSeqReady={historyLoaded}
  onCaughtUp={refetch}
/>
```
