WHAT THE APP IS:
OpenFrame is a remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform for Managed Service Providers - IT companies that maintain client networks under contract. It puts the technician's console on their phone: device status, tickets and chat, monitoring alerts, and actions such as running a script or approving a fix from the AI copilot. Audience: MSP employees, not consumers.

There is no account or organization registration in the app. OpenFrame is sold directly to MSP businesses by contract; the app signs in employees of organizations that already use it, and their accounts are created by the organization's administrator (Guideline 3.1.3(c) Enterprise Services). It is a free companion to that web-based service, with no purchasing inside the app and no calls to action for purchase outside of it (Guideline 3.1.3(f)).

CHANGES SINCE THE PREVIOUS SUBMISSION:
- The Sign Up tab and every in-app organization-creation screen were removed. The app opens on a single Login screen.
- An Apple, Google or Microsoft identity with no OpenFrame account now sees a "No account found" notice explaining that accounts are created by an administrator, with a "Back to Login" button - not a setup form.
- An email address with no account gets the same explanation under the email field.

EXTERNAL SERVICES:
- Auth: Google, Microsoft and Apple OAuth plus our own OpenFrame IdP. Sign in with Apple uses the native ASAuthorizationController sheet. Google, Microsoft and OpenFrame flows run in ASWebAuthenticationSession against our gateway, which does the code exchange server-side. No third-party identity SDK is embedded.
- Push: Firebase Cloud Messaging, the only third-party SDK in the binary. No Analytics or Crashlytics.
- Storage: Google Cloud Storage, via presigned URLs from our backend, for attachments.
- AI assistant: sends conversation context to an LLM provider each customer organization configures (e.g. OpenAI or Anthropic).
- Device management: our backend integrates MeshCentral (Apache 2.0) and Fleet (MIT) on our infrastructure; the app talks only to our gateway.
- Payment processors: none. Analytics: first-party only.

HOW TO SIGN IN:
- To review a workspace containing data: on the Login screen, enter the demo email from the fields above. The app resolves the workspace as you type and shows "Continue with OpenFrame" under the email field. Tap it and enter the same email and password in the sheet that appears. It closes automatically and the dashboard loads.
- "Continue with Apple", "Continue with Google" and "Continue with Microsoft" are enabled as soon as the screen opens; nothing needs to be typed first. They sign in users whose administrator created their account with the same email as that identity. The demo account is not linked to an Apple ID, so with your own Apple ID the app shows "No account found" - this is by design, not an error. "Back to Login" returns to the Login screen.

ACCOUNT DELETION:
Settings -> Profile -> Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and signs the user out everywhere. A workspace OWNER is additionally asked, in the same dialog, to hand ownership to another active member, since a workspace cannot be left ownerless; if the owner is the only member, the dialog explains the two ways to proceed (invite a member first, or email hello@openframe.ai to delete the workspace with the account). The second demo account provided above is a regular member: use it to verify deletion end to end without affecting the primary demo account.

FACE ID:
Off by default - nothing to bypass on a fresh install. After the first sign-in the app offers an optional "Enable Face ID login" dialog. If it is ever enabled, the unlock screen has "Log in another way", which returns to email and password.

NO PURCHASES IN THE APP:
The app contains no in-app purchases and no purchase surfaces of any kind - no plans, prices, trials, upgrade buttons, invoices or links to external payments. There is also no registration and no link to any registration or purchase page; there is no way to start a subscription from the app. OpenFrame is sold to MSP businesses through direct contracts outside of the app, and every payment-related screen is disabled in the native build.

PERMISSIONS:
- Notifications: requested after the first sign-in, not at launch. Allow or deny, neither affects access;
- Camera and microphone: used only when a user attaches a photo or video to a support ticket or knowledge base article (Tickets -> a ticket -> Attach). Never accessed otherwise;
- Face ID: only if the user opts in (above).

USER CONTENT:
Tickets, attachments and chat exist only inside a single MSP's private workspace. Membership is by administrator invitation only - no public content, no discovery, no profiles, no contact between organizations. Support: hello@openframe.ai.

DEVICE SUPPORT:
iPhone only.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES:
No regionally gated features, pricing or content. Access is blocked at network level for sanctions compliance and abuse prevention in: AF, BD, BY, CD, CF, CN, CU, ER, HK, ID, IQ, IR, KP, LB, LY, MD, ML, MM, MO, NG, NI, PK, RU, SD, SO, SS, SY, VE, VN, YE, ZW.
